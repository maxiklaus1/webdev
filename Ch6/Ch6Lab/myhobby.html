My Favorite Hobby: Golf

Golf has been one of my favorite hobbies for as long as I can remember. I enjoy the challenge of improving my game and the focus that is required on every shot. Golf has also taught me patience, discipline, and persistence.

I also enjoy golf because it allows me to spend time outdoors and meet people from different backgrounds. Playing golf competitively has given me the opportunity to travel, compete in tournaments, and create many great memories.
